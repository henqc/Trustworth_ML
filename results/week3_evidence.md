## 3. Scale-matched baseline and kernel width (`results/w4_scale_match.md`, `fig_scale_sweep.png`, `fig_width_sweep.png`)

Question from the pilot: is the synthetic gain just a smaller neighborhood (dependency-aware perturbs at 0.5·Σ, mean distance to query 2.4; independent at 1.0·std, distance 3.1)? Sweep: independent and dependency-aware at scales 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75 / 1.0, RF, n = 500, 3 seeds.

- Synthetic: **no.** At matched distance (dependency-aware 0.5, dist 2.43 vs independent 0.75, dist 2.31) fidelity is 0.80 vs 0.66, paired gain +0.135 ± 0.167, dependency-aware higher in 30 of 30 cases. Shrinking independent's noise further hurts it (scale 0.25: 0.14).
- Adult: not matchable with the scale knob, because the 8 categoricals are still drawn from marginals and dominate the distance (independent ≥ 3.08 vs dependency-aware ≤ 2.39). Closest pair: 0.52 vs −0.39, dependency-aware higher in 14 of 15 cases, stability 0.76 vs 0.44. A fair match needs a categorical keep-probability knob (proposed for week 4).
- Kernel width: adopt the **median distance of the evaluation neighborhood** as the width. It fixes the near-uniform default on Adult (mean kernel weight 0.91 → 0.48) and leaves fidelity and stability unchanged or better; half the median over-concentrates (mean weight 0.07). The dependency-aware minus independent gap barely moves across width rules (synthetic +0.135 / +0.139 / +0.115; Adult +0.92 / +0.87 / +1.02), so the sampler verdict does not depend on the width.

**Interpretation so far.** Scale is ruled out. On synthetic data the no-dependency ablation (0.78) still nearly matches dependency-aware (0.80), so the gain there comes from building samples from real local training neighbors rather than from preserving the x1–x2 correlation. On Adult the dependency constraints are decisive (ablation median 0.15 vs 0.61; stability 0.23 vs 0.76). Training neighbors alone are competitive on Adult (0.57 median, most stable), so week 4 must show what perturbation adds over real records. Working hypothesis for the neighbors baseline's weakness under RF on synthetic: the forest (min_samples_leaf = 2) nearly memorizes training rows; under LR, neighbors and dependency-aware tie (0.91).

## 8. Open issues

- Adult rank stability over 105 one-hot coefficients is noisy even after the correction (many absent levels); feature-group stability (the brief's extension) is the natural remedy.
- "Seen combos" measures empirical support, not impossibility: 19 % of real Adult test records have a categorical tuple unseen in training.
- The sampler's "categorical → numeric compatibility" is exact only for locked partners; strata with fewer than 5 rows fall back to the k-neighbor covariance.
